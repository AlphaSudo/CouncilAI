# ZeroWall 1.02 scoped review preview

Date: October 19, 2026. Application version: **1.2.0**. Status: **software implemented; independent semantic quality and reliable live model compliance remain pending**.

Open a new council, select **Reviewed council**, then **Review method -> Scoped review - 1.02 preview**. Discussion and the original review remain available. Existing conversations retain their frozen interpretation.

## Implemented behavior

- Separate normalized atomic assertions from exact original source spans. The initial profile allows two claims per author, eight per cycle; splitting consumes the same budget and omissions remain disclosed.
- Describe claim kinds and evidence obligations, group related/equivalent/conflicting claims reversibly, retain authors and invalidate stale groups.
- Inspect full openings and repaired replies for reported observations, including passages omitted by extraction. Local cues and model audits are fallible; reported experiments do not become executed evidence.
- Record targeted objections, candidate tests, threshold/baseline/measurement limits, author repairs and explicit revised scopes.
- Compare original objections with current revisions. Separate resolution, partial resolution, missing evidence, new concerns and ordinary risk.
- Permit conditional use of a revised narrower claim with only ordinary remaining concerns, while preserving its partial-review label. Material defects, stale dependencies and missing required evidence still constrain the answer.
- Bind dependencies to reviewed premise revisions. Later support for a changed premise cannot silently refresh an old conclusion.
- Require explicit human scope coverage for supplied evidence; partial evidence cannot certify a whole assertion. Human risk acceptance is not stronger evidence.
- Constrain final answers by current claim revisions, evidence/issue relationships and publication permissions. Invalid finals use a local fallback with a saved reason and immutable publication snapshot.
- Preserve exact v1 policy replay, add v2 packs with source-span checks, retain durable recovery, and restore skipped selected authors only through explicit v2 continuation with a full budget preflight.
- Extend the existing conversation inspector, accessible controls, mobile layout and provenance warnings; prepare a separate offline v1/v2 quality scorer and frozen eight-task-pair design.

No required model calls were added: `2N + 4`, three shared automatic retry attempts, twenty attempts per conversation and one hundred per UTC day per application database remain. OpenRouter stays disabled by default; Token Factory billing verification, its existing-credit ceiling and the $0 additional-payment/$0 GPU limits remain unchanged. Missing provider invoice costs remain unknown.

All failures, the role change and both publications remain saved. The pack reproduces offline. This development run is not an independent quality pilot.

The [additional two-member development case](../validation/reviewed-council-1.02-small.json), `chat_12cece099f29497b`, retained two openings. That run remains unavailable with no admitted atomic claims. Its incomplete pack also reproduces; replay does not imply a completed review.

Actual free-provider availability and semantically faithful constrained synthesis are therefore **not established**. The software handles these failures visibly; it does not make the providers reliable. Existing human controls allow continuation, explicit reassignment or partial finish, subject to the original caps. No paid provider fallback was used. Providers did not report invoice cost for these calls, so this release does not claim a verified zero invoice.

Source fidelity, evidence relevance, test quality, objection correctness and generated prose remain semantic evaluation targets. A correct reference structure is not proof of a correct claim. Some live repaired engineering assertions remain debatable. The eight held-out task pairs have no independent human labels, and the empty scorer reports unknown rates rather than zero errors. Keep the preview opt-in; no general answer-quality improvement is claimed.
