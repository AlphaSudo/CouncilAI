# Scoped review quality pilot

Date: October 19, 2026. Protocol: `generic-quality-pilot-v2`. Status: **tasks and scoring tooling prepared; live paired collection and independent human labels pending**.

This extends [the existing quality protocol](QUALITY_PROTOCOL.md). The earlier Discussion-versus-Reviewed study and its missing labels are not replaced or marked complete.

The [eight candidate task pairs](../../benchmark/review_102_tasks.json) cover software choice, document analysis, quantitative reasoning and planning/criteria, two tasks per category. The canonical task-set hash is `5763ef3b5e4eef4fbad48e1fa74c5f7ffdf3d90dbafc842717425963028fdc63`. Each question and supplied-evidence block has its own hash. The backup diagnostic, smoke queue question and prompt-tuning examples are development material, excluded from this set. Do not tune prompts against subsequently collected held-out outputs or labels; changes require a declared new study version.

For each task, create one v1 and one v2 Reviewed council with the same exact selected model identities, question, supplied evidence and one repair cycle. Freeze the model IDs and prompt/policy versions before collection. Both four-model arms plan twelve successful calls; disclose different claim capacities (four per author in v1, two in the initial v2 profile), tokens, actual participation, failures and retry allocation. Reverse or randomize arm order where practical and record it outside identifying reviewer materials.

The eight-pair design plans 192 successful calls, with up to 48 shared retry attempts across sixteen conversations. Preserve twenty attempts per conversation, one hundred per UTC day in the application database and all upstream quota/funding guards. Use the main attempt ledger; do not reset counters or move the study to a new database to acquire allowance. The manifest/scorer does not automate live dispatch. A smaller study must be declared before seeing labels and reported as smaller.

Use qualified human reviewers who can assess the task and supplied references. Blind version/mode and provider labels where practical, but acknowledge that transcript structure may reveal them. Record expertise, uncertainty, conflicts and disagreements. The software records an operator's human-origin declaration; it cannot authenticate independence. Council self-confidence, another generated review or the author's preferred answer is not independent gold.

Label completed and partial/failed arms. Count material claims and their denominator before rating errors. Record extraction fidelity/coverage, valid scoped proposals retained, unnecessary rejection, material defects missed, objection closures and false closures, correct observation attribution, proposed-test validity, unsupported claims presented as established, goal drift, direct usefulness (1 unusable; 5 directly useful with appropriate uncertainty), and final faithfulness on the same 1-5 scale. Explain uncertain or missing labels.

Critical failures are invented execution evidence, hidden violation of a hard director constraint, false external verification, or loss of a material defect in the final answer. The predeclared product criterion is no such observed failure in the sample and positive paired median usefulness without an increase in material false closure or unsupported-as-established rate. Otherwise report failure or inconclusive evidence. A small descriptive pilot cannot establish population safety, statistical significance, calibrated confidence or universal superiority; do not change defaults automatically.

Populate [the v2 manifest template](../../benchmark/review_quality_v2_template.json). Each pair contains exact question/evidence hashes, both run identities/protocols, actual calls, tokens where available, latency, reported cost or null, and optional human labels. Preserve every eligible task and failed run; do not discard losses. Keep raw exports for audit.

Run `.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts/summarize_review_quality.py benchmark/review_quality_v2_template.json` offline. The empty template reports `independent_quality_pending` and null rates. Synthetic labels remain synthetic and cannot satisfy the human gate. The summary preserves denominators, paired usefulness deltas, missing labels and unknown invoice costs; it never alters publication permissions or provider settings.
